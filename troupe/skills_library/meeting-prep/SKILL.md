# Meeting prep

1. From {meeting} and {attendees}, find each person's role and organisation (official sites, recent
   public work). Skip private or personal details; this is professional preparation only.
2. Summarise the organisation's recent relevant news in three lines.
3. Recall what the owner already knows: past memories and messages mentioning these people.
4. Draft a one-page brief:
   - Purpose of the meeting in one sentence.
   - Who's who (one line each).
   - Three points the owner wants to land.
   - Five questions worth asking, in priority order.
   - Risks or sensitivities to be aware of.
5. Save the brief to /workspace/briefs/ and post the summary.
