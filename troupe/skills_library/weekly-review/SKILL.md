# Weekly review

1. Look back over the last 7 days: room messages, completed tasks, memories and plans.
2. Write four short sections:
   - **Done**: the meaningful outcomes, not the busywork.
   - **Still open**: loose ends, each with a suggested next step.
   - **Coming up**: dates and deadlines in the next 14 days.
   - **Three priorities** for next week, each one line.
3. Offer to schedule follow-ups or hand items to teammates.
Keep it under 250 words. Suggest scheduling this skill for Fridays at 16:00 if it isn't already.
