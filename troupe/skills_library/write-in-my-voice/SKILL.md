# Write in my voice

## Learning the voice
When the owner shares samples of their writing, note: greeting and sign-off habits, sentence length,
formality, warmth, words they use and avoid, punctuation quirks. Save a concise style memory (pinned)
titled "Owner's writing voice".

## Drafting
1. recall("writing voice") before any draft.
2. Write for {recipient} and {purpose}: lead with the point, keep it as short as the owner would.
3. Match their formality to the relationship (a colleague, a friend, a stranger).
4. Never add claims, promises or apologies the owner did not ask for.
5. Present the draft for approval unless the owner said to send.
